About

Ocean-surface mesh coloured by a synthetic numerical-model field (temperature/salinity), overlaid with synthetic current vectors and Argo-float-style in-situ station profiles. Click a station (in the 3D view or the list) to compare its observed depth profile against the model's nearest column. See README for what is real vs. synthetic and how to wire in real data sources.

Region: 40–100°E, 10°S–30°N · Indian Ocean (Arabian Sea / Bay of Bengal) · all data synthetic
